# greet — Specification

#### FR-GREET-01: Greet a person by name
`greet(name)` returns a greeting with the person's name. *Why:* people like to be called by their name.

#### FR-GREET-02: Greet a group of people
`greetAll(names)` greets everyone in one sentence, as a list. *Why:* a group should not get one greeting per person.
